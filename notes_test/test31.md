# Natural Gas Price Time Series Analysis

## Why This Matters  

Natural‑gas prices affect everything from your heating bill to grocery costs (most fertilizers rely on gas‑derived ammonia). The price series wiggles each year because of seasons, weather, and short‑term spikes, but underneath lies a **trend**—the slow rise or fall over months and years.
