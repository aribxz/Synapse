SLIDE 2 — Punch Line
PRESENTER TIP: Deliver this line slowly and with conviction. This is your hook — the one line that should
stick in the audience's mind.
"Engineering Innovation Through Software."
That's our promise — four words that capture everything we stand for.
Engineering has always been the backbone of human progress. But for decades, the tools engineers
rely on have been clunky and expensive. Legacy software companies like Autodesk have dominated
the market — and engineers have had no choice but to pay the price.
EngiNova Technologies is here to change that.
We believe that engineering innovation shouldn't be gated by the size of your budget or the IT
support you can afford. With the power of AI and the cloud, we're bringing world-class engineering
tools to every engineer — whether you're a startup of 3 or a manufacturing firm of 3,000.
This is EngiNova. This is our mission. And this is why we built it.
